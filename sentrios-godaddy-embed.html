Field guide · Construction & utilities

Five generations of jobsite security AI. We're the fifth.

Four generations of AI were never built for the specific needs of security in construction and utilities. When a vendor says "AI," ask which one.

A

A motion-detection alarm is "AI."

B

A box around a person or vehicle is "AI."

C

A model that knows your perimeter, your project team, and what 2 AM activity actually means is "AI."

Same word. Three different products. Thirty years between the first and the last.
01
Pixel triggers · Analog DVRs

Motion-detection alarms

Pixels move; the buzzer fires. A flapping tarp, a possum, a passing semi — every one trips the same alarm. The system has no idea what moved, or why anyone should care.

A buzzer without a brain. Guards learn to ignore what matters.
Gen 1 · 1990–2000s
02
Bounding boxes · Frame-by-frame

Deep-learning object detectors

Recognizes a person, a vehicle, a forklift — one frame at a time. No memory, no sense of what's next. A subcontractor at 6 AM looks identical to a thief at midnight.

A label on a shape. No reasoning about what is a threat.
Gen 2 · 2010s
03
Cloud retrieval · Sub-sampled

Cloud clip & embedding search

Embeddings and retrieval on every Nth frame. Helpful for searching footage after the fact — useless while a perimeter is being cut between two samples.

Forensics finds it tomorrow. The copper left tonight.
Gen 3 · Late 2010s
04
General-purpose VLMs · API-called

General-purpose cloud VLMs

Finally understands the frame — but only in single snapshots, only over a network link, only at API prices. The economics break before the third location.

Brilliant in a frame. Costs run high, quickly.
Gen 4 · Early 2020s
05
Human-like reasoning · Always-on

Construction-trained reasoning VLMs

Always-on at the edge of every camera. Trained on how construction and utility sites actually behave — perimeter rules, roster, shift schedule, talk-down history, what 2 AM activity near a copper-storage trailer really means. The foundation of agentic site security.
