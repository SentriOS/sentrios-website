01
Opportunity

Substation security — more insight, fewer false alarms.

The pain. Substation perimeter analytics are dominated by rule-based CV that fires on motion, wildlife, weather, and shadows. Operators drown in noise, trust collapses, and 911-grade escalations get lost.

The SentriOS difference. A VLM-driven layer that filters alerts using context — who is on site, what is expected, when it occurs — and reasons across video, access control, weather, and schedules. The result is 10–100× fewer alerts, and only the ones worth acting on.

What SentriOS delivers

  • VLM platform that reasons about scenes, intent, and relationships — not bounding boxes
  • Self-learning per-site baselines: worker schedules, vehicle flow, access patterns
  • Native integration with your existing VMS — Milestone, Genetec, Avigilon, Axis, Hanwha
  • On-prem / air-gapped deployment for NERC-CIP and OT-aligned environments
  • RapidSOS connection for verified 911 escalation with GPS + situational context

How we start with you

  • Pick 1–10 substations with higher nuisance alarms for a ≤60-day paid pilot
  • Share your camera + VMS inventory and current false-alarm metrics
  • Define success with your ops team — target ≥95% false-positive reduction
  • Approve on-prem stand-up behind your firewall — we deploy in 2–4 weeks
  • Flexible per-site SaaS expansion once the pilot validates
02
Opportunity

Wildfire & PSPS — AI agents that augment human observers.

The pain. Wildfire and PSPS decisions still depend on human observers and multiple dashboards. Observers are scarce, costly, and slow to dispatch. De-energization decisions affect thousands of customers — utilities need defensible, multi-modal, evidence-based decisions in real time.

The SentriOS difference. VLM-driven visual agents monitor existing or newly installed wildfire / circuit cameras 24/7, describe what they see in plain language, and fuse it with weather, fuel, satellite, and circuit telemetry. Operators ask questions in natural language and get cited, ranked, impact-identified answers with agent reasoning.

Show significant branch movement near circuit 14 in the last 6 hours, ranked by fire risk.
3 events. Highest: 14:22, pole 14-87 — sustained limb contact, wind gusts 34 mph (HRRR), HFTD Tier 3. Cited: 2 frames + circuit telemetry. Recommend patrol before re-energization.

What SentriOS delivers

  • Promptable visual agents — define what to watch for in natural language
  • Multi-modal fusion: video + weather (HRRR/GFS) + satellite + circuit telemetry
  • Custom rules — "alert if smoke within 500m AND wind gusts > 30 mph"
  • Event search across months of footage, in plain language
  • Live wildfire / PSPS dashboard with operator chat and audit trail

How we start with you

  • Identify the wildfire / PSPS ops team and one priority circuit or HFTD zone
  • Connect existing or new wildfire / circuit feeds — any camera, any VMS
  • Interface with your ADMS / OMS / weather / fuel feeds — all inside your IT environment
  • Run a 60-day live pilot during high fire activity, measured on coverage and PSPS decision quality & latency

Opportunity

Co-develop tailored VLMs for asset inspection.

The pain. Every utility has volumes of drone, helicopter, climber, and phone imagery — and single-task ML models that each need thousands of new labels per asset class. Adding a modality or defect type means starting over, and infraction calls stay inconsistent with limited rulebook citation.

The SentriOS difference. We co-develop a Vision-Language Model on your data, behind your firewall. It warm-starts to Day-1 parity with your existing model on your labels, then compounds 10× as it ingests new imagery and assimilates your O&M manuals and standards — with every infraction cross-referenced to the relevant rule.

What SentriOS delivers

  • Pretrained multi-utility VLM + 120K+ labeled asset images for warm-start
  • Agentic ingestion of your O&M procedures, NESC/IEEE, and internal standards
  • One model across modalities — drone RGB, thermal, helicopter, climber, phone
  • On-prem training + inference; you own the weights and the audit trail (SHA-256 chain)
  • Active-learning loop — every SME correction compounds accuracy
  • 10× target reduction in missed P1/P2 defects within a 16-week engagement

How we start with you

  • Pick one circuit or substation cluster for a 12–16 week pilot
  • Provide inspection imagery archives and your incumbent ML/CV baseline
  • Commit ~4 hrs/wk of SME time for rulebook validation
  • Deploy on a GPU server behind your firewall — we bring the spec
  • Establish Day-1 parity benchmark and KPIs up front; expand once parity is hit
  • You keep the weights, the data, and the audit trails — no vendor lock-in
